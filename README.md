# 📊 Customer Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Customer Behavior Analysis** project using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to explore customer purchasing patterns, sales performance, revenue distribution, subscription behavior, and demographic trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer behavior and purchasing patterns to understand customer segments, sales performance, revenue distribution, subscription behavior, and demographic trends.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

### 📊 Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard providing a comprehensive view of customer demographics, purchasing behavior, sales performance, subscription status, and revenue trends.

![Customer Behavior Analysis Dashboard](images/customer_behavior_dashboard.png)

> **Dashboard Features:** KPI tracking • Customer segmentation • Sales & revenue analysis • Subscription analysis • Age-group analysis • Interactive filters

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI dashboard provides:

* **Customer Count**
* **Average Purchase Amount**
* **Average Review Rating**
* Customer distribution by **Subscription Status**
* Sales and revenue by **Product Category**
* Sales and revenue by **Age Group**
* Customer analysis by **Gender**
* Analysis of **Shipping Type**
* Interactive filtering by customer and transaction attributes

---

## 🔍 Key Analytical Areas

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

Analyze customers based on demographic and behavioral attributes such as age group, gender, and subscription status.

### 💰 Sales & Revenue Analysis

Compare sales performance and revenue contribution across product categories and customer segments.

### ⭐ Customer Experience

Analyze review ratings alongside purchasing behavior to understand customer experience.

### 📦 Shipping Behavior

Explore customer preferences across different shipping types.

### 🔄 Subscription Analysis

Analyze customer distribution by subscription status and identify purchasing patterns across subscription segments.
